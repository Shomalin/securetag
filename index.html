How it works

  1. Attach

    Put a QR sticker or NFC keychain on your item.

  2. Scan

    A finder taps or scans the tag with any phone. No app needed.

  3. Reply

    You get the message in your dashboard and arrange the return.

Your details stay private

The tag only shows the item name and a note you choose. Contact details are never on the tag or on the page a finder sees.
